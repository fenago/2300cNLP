          How it works

          What am I looking at?

          A short guide to the ideas behind this page, in plain language.

          What is Word2Vec?

          Word2Vec is a small neural network that learns a list of numbers (a vector) for every word. It reads lots of text and practices one simple game: given a word, guess the words around it. To win, words that show up in similar contexts (like coffee and tea) need similar vectors.

          Nobody tells it what words mean. Meaning emerges from usage: "you shall know a word by the company it keeps."

          Cosine similarity

          To compare two words we measure the angle between their vectors. Pointing the same way gives 1.0, unrelated is near 0, and opposite is −1. Length is ignored, so a rare word and a common word can still match.

          cos(a, b) = (a · b) / (‖a‖ ‖b‖)

          High similarity means "used in similar contexts". That includes synonyms, but also opposites (hot and cold appear in the same kinds of sentences).

          4

          Why the maps are approximate

          Screens are 2D, but each word lives in 100 dimensions. We use PCA (principal component analysis) to find the 2 or 3 directions along which the shown words differ most, and flatten everything onto them.

          That is a lossy projection: two words can look close on the map but be far apart in the full space. Trust the similarity scores in the list; use the maps for intuition. The legend shows how much of the variation each view keeps.
